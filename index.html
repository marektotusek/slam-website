// Know what worked

We track every video after it goes live.

Most studios hand over files and leave. Every SLAM package includes a 30-day results report. Retainer and series clients get a live dashboard.

WHAT YOU GIVE US
View-only access to your YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and Google Analytics. You approve it on each platform's own screen. We never need your passwords.
HOW LONG SETUP TAKES
About a week after delivery. Reporting starts once access is in place, so send it within five days of delivery.
WHAT WE CAN MEASURE
Views, watch time, reach, engagement and follower growth on each platform, plus clicks and form fills on your site through a tracked link for every video.
WHAT WE CAN'T
Each platform decides what it shares, and someone who watches on Instagram then calls your sales team two weeks later won't show up as a click. We report direction, not exact attribution.
